# GeoNames: notice

`src/server/geo/cities.json` is generated by `scripts/gen-cities.mjs` from GeoNames `cities15000.txt`
(https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/), read on 3 November 2026. It keeps city names of
100,000+ people, and alternate Latin-script names of 300,000+, mapped to their ISO country code;
a name two countries share is dropped unless the larger city is at least four times the size of
the next.
